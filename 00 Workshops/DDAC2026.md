Abstract  
This workshop demonstrates core elements of the modern Transformer-based Large Language Models (LLMs) by hand using a paper worksheet, a spreadsheet template and a simple example. With each step implemented as a spreadsheet formula in a cell, participants can experience and visually witness how values are computed and how they flow through the network structure. Key concepts include tokens, embeddings, dot product, softmax, attention and transformer. Basic understanding of deep learning (i.e. artificial neural networks) is assumed.

This exercise is based on Chapter 10 of Dr. Chung's textbook [AI for Business](https://www.prospectpressvt.com/textbooks/chung-ai-for-business).  

Dr. Chung starts the presentation with this [slide deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1vi0rYWIiRO6F_YW9VdTjJ2HrXxTEhozIImFUoEPfiTI/edit?slide=id.p15#slide=id.p15) (about 10 minutes)

Then participants will walk through this worksheet together (about 20 minutes).
